# Sokoban

The [game sokoban described on wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoban) is a good introduction and resource locator. 

We will describe the absolute minimum of knowledge here.

## Gameplay

You are a storage worker. You push crates to places. 

Each puzzle is an area of inside with a number of designated storage places. There must be enough crates to fill every storage place.
